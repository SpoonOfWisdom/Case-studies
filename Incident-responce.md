# Worksheet: Incident response

## Activity summary:
Analyze 5 different scenarios and analyze which phases of the incident response plan were utilized, give a grade (A-F) on how the incident was handled, suggest any additional corrective measures, and analyze their disaster recovery/ business continuity.

## Takeaway
Having an **IRP** as well as safety measures that **prevent**, **contain**, **mitigate**, **recover**, and **prepare** for an attack drastically reduce the potential damage that could occur from an attack. Having multi-layered defense systems helps contain any attacks by being able to easily separate them from other systems and prevent further spread.
